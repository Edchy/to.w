Scope: homepage, jewelry collection and detail pages, shared footer, and course card. Modes: Experience on the homepage and gallery-facing surfaces; Persuade on product and course actions.

Audience and job: visitors should encounter the breadth of Tove's practice, then move deliberately to the full jewelry collection, a specific object, a course, or direct contact.

Direction: a tough, clean photographic editorial system. Real images create the rhythm; square-edged paper and ink surfaces, decisive dark fields, restrained red, and the approved Gothic body stack create structure. The homepage is a two-to-four-column masonry mix rather than a second catalog or a separate gallery. Product pages pair one large real photograph with a compact, dark order panel. The course becomes a defined card-like object.

Memorable moment: the homepage opens with a highly visible course invitation, then breaks into a dense photographic flow where the few purchasable objects are distinguished by title, price, and description.

Constraints: preserve factual copy, email ordering, Netlify form behavior, two-column mobile collections, the uppercase two-line Avenir-led wordmark, and real assets. Remove isolated transparent ring renders, not photographic AVIFs. No ecommerce conventions, generic rounded cards, or invented claims.
